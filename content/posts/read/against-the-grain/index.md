**Title**: Against the Grain
<br>
**Author**: James C. Scott
{{< /alert >}}

**Grain = tax = slavery. Welcome to civilization.**

The author challenges the idea that the state and agriculture represented natural progress. He shows how grain, taxation and coercion created hierarchies that oppressed the majority, and how “barbarians” were often refugees from the oppression of the states. A provocative, well-founded and necessary read for anyone who believes in the linear narrative of history.

_For you if you…_
